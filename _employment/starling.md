## Responsibilities
- Shaped onboarding API requirements directly with partner clients, product and compliance teams
- Adapted customer re-verification workflows to each partner's requirements
- Wrote and maintained API documentation for client integrations
- Built transaction freezes and account holds to support urgent fraud investigations
- Took part in incident response, releases and production support

## Key contributions
- Designed a flexible domain model for the SaaS template, covering core banking functions, account structures and international addresses
- Added dynamic, configurable fraud rules and thresholds, so the fraud team could respond quickly to new threats
- Spotted recurring design issues, such as tightly coupled services and overlapping domain models, and proposed fixes for long-term maintainability
- Wrote onboarding guides that helped new engineers become independent sooner

## What I gained

- Balancing urgent operational needs against long-term design
- Confidence working directly with clients and non-technical teams
